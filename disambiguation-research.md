# Plaza Colón — research notes for English disambiguation

## Córdoba target place

The former Turismo Córdoba page currently returns a not-found page, so it cannot be used as a live citation in the new comparison section. Search results and previously collected public descriptions consistently associate the 1955 planting redesign with architect **Carlos David**, not Carlos Thays. The new section must therefore not call Carlos Thays the designer. It will describe the square as a late-19th-century public square with Parisian cast-iron fountain and lamps only where phrased as heritage elements already documented in the guide; it will not claim that all sculpture is bronze.

## Madrid comparator

Madrid Tourism identifies Plaza de Colón as the site of a monument to Christopher Columbus in the centre of a fountain. Its direct page did not expose full article text in extraction; the comparison will use the broad, stable distinction “monument-and-flag civic landmark” without unverifiable measurements or superlatives.

## San Juan and Mar del Plata comparators

Discover Puerto Rico describes Plaza Colón in San Juan as being at the landward entrance to the walled city of Old San Juan, with a Columbus monument in its centre. Turismo Mar del Plata is the municipality’s tourism portal and confirms Mar del Plata’s destination context, including mobility and visitor services; the comparison will call the local Plaza Colón a coastal city square and avoid unverified claims about particular statues, fountains or its precise relationship to the beach.

## Editorial decision

The future English block will say the site is focused on Plaza Colón in Córdoba, Argentina, at Av. Colón in Alberdi. It will contrast purpose and urban setting, not rank places or imply that similarly named plazas are affiliated.
