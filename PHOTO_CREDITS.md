# Photograph source

`public/images/plaza-2022.jpg` is a display derivative of [Plaza Colón, Córdoba, Argentina.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Plaza_Col%C3%B3n,_C%C3%B3rdoba,_Argentina.jpg), by **Santi Cba**, photographed 18 September 2022, dedicated under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The source describes the view from Avellaneda street towards María Auxiliadora Parish. It is a historical reference, not a current inspection.
